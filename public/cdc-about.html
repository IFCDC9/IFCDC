WHO WE ARE

One mission.
Many ways forward.

IFCDC is a New Jersey nonprofit organization focused on community development. Our work brings together youth and mentorship, outreach, economic opportunity, housing initiatives, education, media, and software.

We believe a strong foundation gives people more room to create, participate, and grow. Our divisions connect community purpose with practical tools and creative services.

Programs, partnerships, and services develop over time. Contact IFCDC to discuss current availability and how we can work together.
