Public website terms · Updated October 26, 2026

Program and service enquiries

Information describes IFCDC’s work and areas of focus. Availability, eligibility, staffing, scope, and schedules are confirmed directly with IFCDC. An enquiry is not a reservation, booking, service agreement, or promise of assistance. Paid service work requires agreed scope, price, timing, and delivery terms.

Donations and memberships

Donations are voluntary gifts with no goods or services provided in exchange. Community Supporter memberships include project updates and a monthly newsletter and renew monthly at the amount you approve until canceled. Cancel future renewals through PayPal Automatic Payments or the receipt control in the same browser session. Cancellation does not automatically refund past charges. We do not promise every payment is tax deductible. Contact service@ifcdc.org about incorrect or duplicate charges and refund requests.

Careers and volunteering

Submitting interest is not an employment application for an advertised position, a job offer, or a guarantee of paid work. Formal recruitment and onboarding, where applicable, take place through a separate process.

Publishing and other services

Amazon manages purchases and fulfillment through its own listing terms. The Barbers App operates through its own booking and payment terms. Third-party links do not imply that IFCDC controls their availability or policies.
