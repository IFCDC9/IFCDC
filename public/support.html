A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Where does my support go?

Your support helps sustain IFCDC’s community work, creative projects, and the design, development, and maintenance of software systems that serve our mission.

Can I choose my membership amount?

Yes. Choose $10, $20, $40, or $60 a month, or enter your own amount between $1 and $1,000. Your approved amount renews monthly until you cancel. Membership includes project updates and a monthly newsletter.

How do I cancel a membership?

Cancel future renewals through PayPal Automatic Payments, or use the cancellation control on your receipt in this browser. Cancellation does not automatically refund past payments. Contact service@ifcdc.org for help.

What payment methods can I use?

When online giving is open, PayPal shows eligible payment methods for your account and location. One-time checkout can offer PayPal, cards, Venmo, or Pay Later; monthly membership options can differ. Availability is confirmed at checkout.

Clear terms. Meaningful support.

Donations & memberships

Donations are voluntary gifts with no goods or services provided in exchange. Community Supporter memberships include project updates and a monthly newsletter. We do not promise that every payment is tax deductible. Membership renews monthly at the amount you approve until canceled.

Cancellations & payment questions

Cancel a membership anytime to stop future renewals. Cancellation does not automatically refund past charges. For an incorrect or duplicate charge or a refund request, contact service@ifcdc.org with the receipt ID. Service terms are agreed in your quote; Amazon manages book order questions and returns.

Your information

PayPal processes payment details; IFCDC does not collect card numbers here. We retain payment references, membership status, and project requests to operate these services. Your membership email is used for updates and the newsletter. Please do not include financial credentials or sensitive personal records in a project request.
